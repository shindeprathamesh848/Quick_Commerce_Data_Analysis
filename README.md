# Quick_Commerce_Data_Analysis

Blinkit • Zepto • Swiggy Instamart
📝 Project Description

Quick Commerce (Q-Commerce) platforms such as Blinkit, Zepto, and Swiggy Instamart focus on delivering groceries and daily essentials within minutes. Due to extremely high order volumes, short delivery timelines, and intense competition, data analysis is essential for improving operational efficiency and customer experience.
In this project, I analyzed transactional data inspired by Blinkit, Zepto, and Swiggy Instamart using Microsoft Excel to extract insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, order trends, and delivery efficiency.
The goal is to convert raw data into actionable business insights that can help quick commerce companies make data-driven decisions.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze sales and revenue performance across Blinkit, Zepto, and Swiggy Instamart
Identify top-performing products and categories
Understand customer purchasing behavior
Analyze order trends across different time periods
Evaluate delivery performance and efficiency
Build visual dashboards for easy interpretation
Provide business recommendations for quick commerce platforms

Created derived fields such as:

📌 Key Insights

Blinkit and Zepto show higher order volumes during peak hours

Swiggy Instamart has relatively higher average order value

A small number of categories contribute majority of total revenue

Repeat customers generate significantly higher revenue

Delivery delays increase during high-demand time slots

💡 Business Recommendations

Optimize inventory for high-demand categories across platforms

Improve delivery staffing during peak order hours

Monitor delivery SLAs to reduce delays
